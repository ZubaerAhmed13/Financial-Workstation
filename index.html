Earnings Quality & Cash Conversion

Analytical signal, not a fraud accusation. Earnings-quality analysis compares profit to cash and working-capital trends. Divergence is flagged as a "potential quality concern requiring investigation" — never automatically as manipulation.

Forecast Builder

Use A = Actual, E = Estimate, L = Long-term / terminal assumption. Historical and forecast periods are always visually separated.

Valuation Cross-Check & Consensus

Reverse DCF — Market-Implied Expectations

What must the market believe for the current price to be justified? This inverts the DCF: instead of estimating value from assumptions, it finds the growth/margin assumptions implied by today's price.

Assumption Center

Single source of truth. Every assumption shown here is the master value used across the financial model, valuation, credit, risk, stress and thesis modules. Changing an assumption here and re-running the full analysis propagates it downstream. Analyst overrides are always shown and never silently overwrite the system estimate.

Analysis Library

Persistent, local only. Saved analyses are stored in this browser. They are not uploaded anywhere. Loading a saved analysis restores its full model, thesis, research and results.

Review Queue

Actionable alerts. This queue aggregates issues across modules — stale data, thesis deterioration, covenant status, valuation sensitivity, credit risk, overrides and model disagreement — sorted by severity. Each item links to the module where it can be reviewed.

Strategy Backtesting

Not a forecast. Backtests use historical prices and are subject to look-ahead, survivorship and selection bias. Transaction costs and slippage are applied. Results never guarantee future performance.
