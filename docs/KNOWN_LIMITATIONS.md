# Known Limitations

These limitations are intentionally explicit. They are not silently presented as institutional-grade precision.

1. **Bond settlement conventions:** the current workstation bond workflow is period-based. It does not yet provide full settlement-date clean/dirty pricing, accrued-interest schedules, ex-coupon handling, business-day calendars, or selectable Actual/Actual, 30/360, Actual/360, etc. day-count conventions.
2. **Bond optionality:** option-adjusted spread, callable/putable bond trees, key-rate duration and full term-structure bootstrapping remain outside the certified core.
3. **Residual income:** the certified valuation-matrix path converts total residual-income equity value to per-share value before comparison with market price, but the model remains simplified. It assumes clean-surplus mechanics, payout ratio 0 and a simplified terminal-ROE convention because the existing UI does not expose a full payout/book-value forecast.
4. **Risk frequency inference:** the certified imported-price risk path infers periods/year from median timestamp spacing and supports daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, semiannual and annual cadence. Sparse, irregular, mixed-frequency or bad timestamps can still make cadence inference ambiguous. When cadence cannot be inferred, the legacy 252 periods/year fallback is explicitly tagged as a fallback rather than silently presented as observed frequency.
5. **Risk annualization:** annualized return, volatility, Sharpe and Sortino depend on the inferred/selected periods/year convention. Arithmetic-period means and square-root volatility scaling are analytical approximations and do not make non-i.i.d. return series stationary.
6. **Recovery unit:** the current price-history UI reports recovery in observations. Calendar-day recovery is available in certified primitives when timestamps are supplied, but the legacy card does not render both measures.
7. **Market data:** the workstation remains offline-first and does not provide a live institutional market-data or custodian feed. Imported/user-entered data quality directly affects results, and certification does not reconcile user data to an external exchange, custodian or accounting source.
8. **IRR multiple roots:** the certified IRR returns a bracketed root where one is found. Non-conventional cash flows can have multiple economically valid roots; users should inspect sign changes and NPV profiles.
9. **XIRR day basis:** XIRR uses a 365-day year convention in the certified core and production runtime.
10. **VaR / Expected Shortfall:** `RiskCreditCore` validates inputs, supports arbitrary valid parametric confidence levels, interpolated historical quantiles, fractional-tail ES and Monte Carlo denominator safety. Results remain model- and sample-dependent. Parametric normality, historical stationarity and Monte Carlo path assumptions can all be violated in real markets, so the methods can disagree materially and should not be treated as loss guarantees.
11. **Small-sample tails:** very high-confidence historical VaR/ES computed from short samples has limited empirical information. The core avoids fabricating observations but cannot create tail evidence that is absent from the data.
12. **Accounting framework comparability:** IFRS/US-GAAP awareness and accounting-quality tooling do not replace an accounting-policy review of issuer filings.
13. **Specialized accounting scores:** the shared ratio layer and certified Altman paths are hardened for zero/missing semantics. Other specialized scoring frameworks retain their own assumptions unless explicitly routed through a certified core.
14. **Altman models:** Altman Z/Z-prime are heuristic screening models. Coefficients/thresholds depend on the selected variant and population; a computed score is not a bankruptcy prediction or credit rating.
15. **Credit PD table:** the built-in rating table is an illustrative cumulative-PD model input, not a live agency transition/default study. Exact horizons are returned directly and intermediate horizons are linearly interpolated; the certified path intentionally refuses extrapolation outside the configured horizon range.
16. **Merton structural model:** the certified solver validates inputs and requires convergence of equity-value and volatility residuals before returning PD. The model still depends heavily on market-equity value, equity volatility, debt boundary, risk-free rate, horizon and lognormal asset-value assumptions. Structural PD is a model estimate, not an observed default probability.
17. **ECL:** certified production ECL validates PD, recovery and EAD, preserves legitimate zeroes and leaves missing EAD missing. It remains a point-in-time `PD × LGD × EAD` calculation. IFRS 9/CECL staging, lifetime term structures, macro overlays, discounting and contractual cash-flow timing require a more detailed credit-loss engine.
18. **JavaScript numeric model:** calculations use IEEE-754 double precision. This is appropriate for analytical modeling but not a decimal-ledger/accounting-posting engine.
19. **Three-statement forecast assumptions:** `FinancialModelCore` certifies forecast mechanics, debt aggregation, cash-flow coupling, working-capital mechanics, reconciliation behavior and zero preservation. It remains simplified: there is no full tax-loss carryforward engine, lease schedule, deferred-tax roll-forward, share-based-compensation roll-forward, acquisition accounting, revolver/minimum-cash financing solver, circular interest calculation, or detailed retained-earnings/dividend module.
20. **Opening balance sheet:** the certified model deliberately does not hide an unbalanced opening balance sheet with a cash plug. If starting assets do not equal liabilities plus equity, the difference is carried and disclosed; users must reconcile source inputs for a balanced forecast.
21. **EBITDA margin semantics:** detailed COGS, SG&A and R&D assumptions determine modeled EBITDA. The separate EBITDA-margin series is a target/diagnostic; a material mismatch generates a caution instead of silently forcing the assumptions to agree.
22. **Debt schedule:** certified debt modeling supports simultaneous instruments with fixed rates and fixed annual repayment/amortization. It does not yet model floating curves, refinancing, PIK interest, mandatory/optional sweeps, fees, covenant cures or instrument-specific maturity dates.
23. **Stress testing:** the certified stress path preserves valid zero assumptions, intended revenue fallback and zero PD. Scenarios remain deterministic DCF sensitivity overlays, not stochastic macro balance-sheet simulations, historical replay, liquidity stress, reverse stress testing or regulatory capital stress testing.
24. **Portfolio covariance model:** the hardened legacy portfolio path validates weights/returns/volatility and removes hidden volatility fallbacks, but currently uses a single fixed pairwise correlation of **0.4** rather than a user-supplied covariance matrix or time-series-estimated covariance structure. Separate promoted optimizer/risk-contribution routes have their own explicitly supplied correlation inputs and still depend on the quality/validity of those inputs.
25. **Portfolio stress model:** asset-class stress remains a simplified heuristic mapping. It is not a full repricing engine for bonds, derivatives, options, FX, convexity, term structures, credit migration, liquidity or nonlinear instruments.
26. **Comparable valuation:** the corrected path converts the company-to-peer multiple ratio into implied fair price as `current price / relative multiple`. This is meaningful only when company and peer multiples use consistent definitions and the peers are genuinely comparable. Peer selection/accounting normalization remain user/data responsibilities.
27. **Value-driver sensitivity:** hardened drivers remove truthy defaults and the fabricated `baseVal || 1` denominator. They remain one-variable-at-a-time deterministic DCF sensitivities rather than joint distributions or probabilistic attribution.
28. **Browser smoke scope:** CI is green across pinned Chromium, Firefox and WebKit desktop engines plus Chromium 390×844 mobile. The primary pass opens all 42 discovered views, activates all 59 tabs, reports no page/console errors or external HTTP(S) requests, and executes **17 production runtime calculations**. A separate final workstation-route suite executes **15 routed checks** on each of the same four targets. This is broad runtime/routing smoke, not exhaustive every-input proof.
29. **Mobile coverage:** the 390×844 Chromium pass verifies overflow and menu/sidebar interaction in headless automation. It is not certification on physical Android/iOS devices, touch keyboards, orientation changes, OS accessibility settings or every mobile browser build.
30. **Pinned browser environment:** browser certification uses Playwright 1.55.0 and its pinned headless Chromium/Firefox/WebKit engines. Later browser releases or platform-specific differences require fresh verification.
31. **Monte Carlo model:** `SimulationBacktestCore` uses deterministic seeded geometric Brownian motion for the certified `MonteCarlo.run` route. Deterministic seeding makes tests reproducible; it does not make future prices predictable. GBM assumes a simplified continuous lognormal process with configured drift/volatility and does not model stochastic volatility, jumps, changing regimes, fat tails, liquidity, path-dependent corporate actions, or parameter-estimation uncertainty unless separately added upstream.
32. **Monte Carlo output interpretation:** zero volatility and other edge cases are handled deterministically, but simulation probabilities remain conditional on the supplied model and assumptions. They are not real-world probability guarantees or investment advice.
33. **Backtest execution chronology:** the certified route prevents current-close lookahead by ensuring a signal derived from a completed bar cannot earn the return already realized on that same bar. This does not prove every upstream signal generator is free of lookahead, survivorship bias, revised data, future-known fundamentals, or data leakage; those inputs remain the caller's responsibility.
34. **Backtest transaction costs:** certified backtesting deducts configured turnover-based cost/slippage when turnover occurs and charges two-sided turnover for direct position flips. It remains a simplified execution model and does not simulate order books, partial fills, spreads that vary intraday, market impact, borrow availability/fees, venue latency, taxes, commissions by tier, or nonlinear execution constraints unless represented in the configured costs.
35. **Backtest cadence:** annualization is inferred from supplied timestamps. Sparse, duplicated, irregular, incorrect or non-representative timestamps can make the inferred cadence differ from the intended strategy frequency. Annualized strategy/benchmark comparisons are only as reliable as that time basis and the input series.
36. **Workstation ledger scope:** `WorkstationLedgerCore` hardens the explicitly routed ledger/value/cash/unrealized calculations, but it is not a double-entry accounting system, tax-lot engine, custodian reconciliation service, corporate-actions processor, or tax-reporting engine. Source transaction completeness and FX/price quality remain external responsibilities.
37. **Optimizer/model dependence:** promoted optimizer, risk-contribution, attribution and performance routes validate and normalize the inputs they own, but outputs remain dependent on expected returns, volatility/correlation assumptions, benchmark data, and historical sample quality. Mathematical optimization does not remove estimation error or guarantee investable portfolios.
38. **Legacy monolith scope:** every current static-audit regex lead in the deployable monolith is inventoried and context-classified, but broad matches are not equivalent to defects. Certification applies strictly to `FinanceCore`, `FinancialModelCore`, `LegacyCalculationCore`, `RiskCreditCore`, `WorkstationCalculationCore`, `WorkstationLedgerCore`, `SimulationBacktestCore`, the production calculation paths explicitly mapped/tested through them, and documented narrow build-boundary fixes. Legacy UI, rendering, orchestration and composition wrappers are not automatically independently algorithm-certified merely because they delegate to certified calculations. Other unrouted legacy analytical helpers remain outside the formal boundary until separately tested and promoted.
39. **Static-audit count:** the current run-87 deployable artifact contains **451** broad pattern leads, **231** conservatively categorized as calculation-sensitive or algorithmic-review, with **0 unclassified**. These counts are audit/review inventory, not a claim of 231 remaining bugs.
